# TG Apps Operating Guide

## 1. Who We Are
- Founder-led, boutique digital product studio based in São Paulo, Brazil.
- Focus on nearshore collaboration with North American startups/agencies **and** clients worldwide.
- We intentionally stay small (2–3 concurrent products) so decision-makers collaborate directly with the people designing, coding, and deploying.
- Core strengths: React/Next.js web apps, React Native mobile apps, pragmatic Node/Python backends, UX discovery, performance/security hardening, and hands-on support plans.
- We value honest scoping, lean collaboration, sustainable pace, and full ownership from kickoff through iteration.

## 2. Ideal Clients
1. **MVP-focused startups** – need full-stack support (database, auth, websites, app stores, APIs) to validate ideas fast.
2. **Cost-sensitive teams** – SMBs or agencies that must ship multiple initiatives without hiring an entire in-house squad; we deliver for a fraction of the cost while preserving senior quality.

## 3. Core Message & Positioning
- “Build digital products with speed and integrity from Latin America.”
- Promise: founder involvement, transparent budgets, flexible billing (monthly or per-task), and rapid communication (≤ 1 business day).
- NDAs are common; we share sanitized references privately after understanding context.
- “You thought about it, we’re already building” – demonstrates urgency and anti-bureaucracy mindset.

## 5. Engagement Lifecycle
### 5.1 Before Onboarding
1. **Inquiry** – Client shares goals via website form or email.
2. **Zero-cost call** (within 1 business day):
   - Explain how we help, show sanitized examples, outline security practices.
   - Discuss collaboration model, tooling, payment preferences (retainer or per-task).
   - Align on scope, timeline, and success criteria. No bureaucracy—meeting can be scheduled immediately.
3. **Scoping package** – Written summary of outcomes, milestones, pricing, and 2–3 product concurrency constraints to set expectations.

### 5.2 During Build
1. **Kickoff** – Founders stay involved; we integrate with client rituals and tools.
2. **Cadence** – Weekly or biweekly planning, demos, and deploys. Transparent backlog tools and written updates keep budgets visible.
3. **Delivery** – We handle end-to-end work: infrastructure, code, UX, QA, analytics, app store submissions, etc.
4. **Security & Quality** – NDAs honored, sanitized assets shared when needed, monitoring and logging baked in.

### 5.3 After Launch
1. **Care plans** – Same builders run hypercare, bug fixes, and feature iterations.
2. **Training & handoff** – Documentation kits, team onboarding, knowledge transfer sessions.
3. **Ongoing partnership** – We remain available to pick up new scopes when the roadmap expands.

## 6. Collaboration Principles
- **Honest Scoping:** Only accept work the founders can personally oversee.
- **Lean Collaboration:** Plug into client tools/process; avoid new bureaucracy.
- **Sustainable Pace:** Limit concurrent builds (2–3) to keep attention high.
- **Ownership:** Founders review every deliverable and stay until the project meets goals.

## 7. Communication & Responsiveness
- Replies within one business day (email/WhatsApp).
- Availability: Weekdays 9am–6pm BRT (EST+1 overlap).
- Meetings: short, outcome-focused; we hate unnecessary calls.
- Status artifacts: weekly written reports, backlog snapshots, and demo recordings.

## 8. Payment & Budgeting
- Flexible structures: monthly retainers or per-task/work-package billing, depending on client need.
- Budgets typically expressed in USD ranges (5k–100k+), but local currency options available when needed.
- Emphasis on transparent costs aligned with startups/SMB realities.

## 9. Security & Confidentiality
- Majority of work under NDA.
- Share anonymized architecture/code snippets post-intro call.
- Provide references willing to speak after NDA or mutual agreement.
- Deployment pipelines include logging, monitoring, and access controls.

## 10. SEO & Messaging Guidelines
- Target keywords: nearshore development studio, React/Next.js, React Native agency, Latin America developers, product sprints, North America software partner.
- Always emphasize truthful metrics (no inflated client/project counts).
- Highlight fast call scheduling, bilingual delivery (EN/PT), and North America-friendly overlap.

## 11. Key Copy Points (EN & PT)
- **Hero**: “Nearshore Product Team / Time nearshore de produtos” – focus on speed, integrity, Brazil-based, serving North America + world.
- **Stats**: “Founder-led delivery, max 2 concurrent builds, replies within 1 business day” (EN) / “Fundadores no dia a dia, máx. 2 projetos, resposta em até 1 dia útil” (PT).
- **Portfolio**: Mention NDA reality; invite prospects to request private walkthroughs.
- **Contact CTA**: Offer zero-cost call to discuss process, security, examples, timelines, and budgets. “Pensou em algo, já estamos construindo.”

## 12. Posture with Clients
- Always be transparent about capacity and timelines.
- Encourage asynchronous updates; demos are weekly or biweekly.
- Document every scope decision and share budgets in writing.
- Post-launch, maintain the same level of accountability as during build.
