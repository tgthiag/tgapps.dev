Recommended Clutch Description
TG Apps builds custom software for founders, startups, SMBs, agencies, and teams that need high-output execution without the overhead of a large agency or a full internal product team.

Our main difference is output efficiency: clients get more software delivery per dollar through a compact team model, low bureaucracy, intelligent allocation, and direct access to senior delivery leadership.

Our work includes mobile apps, web platforms, custom CRM systems, internal tools, dashboards, backend APIs, databases, workflow automation, AI and LLM integrations, app rescue, release execution, and born-global apps with multilingual UX and contextual localization.

Clients work directly with senior delivery leadership while our compact core team and project-based collaborators are allocated intelligently according to project needs, complexity, and required expertise. The process stays low-bureaucracy but structured: we follow the agreed client plan, define scope and acceptance criteria, ship weekly demos, flag risks and tradeoffs, and provide clean handoff with repositories, credentials, documentation, and runbooks when applicable.

TG Apps is a strong fit for companies replacing spreadsheets and disconnected tools, founders trying to finish and launch an app, startups needing product delivery before hiring a full engineering team, and SMBs that need CRM, scheduling, customer portals, employee apps, dashboards, integrations, or operational systems built around how the business actually works.

We operate from Brazil with remote delivery for US and international clients, offering English communication, timezone overlap, strong value for the budget, and practical software ownership. Our cost advantage comes from low overhead, direct communication, and intelligent allocation, not from cutting delivery discipline. We are not positioned as a large enterprise consultancy or a vague staffing vendor. We are an execution partner for clear, practical software builds.
